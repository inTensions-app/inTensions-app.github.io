# Product

## Why This Project Exists

Eudaimonia Labs LLC is a small, privacy-first mobile app company. This website serves as the **central hub** for the company — hosting privacy policies (legally required), landing pages for each app, and an "about" page communicating the company's design philosophy.

The domain `eudaimonialabs.org` replaces the old `intensions.app` domain that was used exclusively for the inTensions app.

## Problems It Solves

1. **Legal compliance**: App stores (Google Play) require a privacy policy URL. Each app must have its own at a stable, public URL.
2. **Discoverability**: Users searching for apps or Eudaimonia Labs need a place to land.
3. **Trust**: A polished company site with a clear philosophy builds trust with privacy-conscious users.

## How It Should Work

- `eudaimonialabs.org/` — Company landing page: design philosophy, links to both apps
- `eudaimonialabs.org/intensions` — inTensions app landing page (styled like the app's UI)
- `eudaimonialabs.org/intensions/privacy` — inTensions privacy policy
- `eudaimonialabs.org/verbatim` — Verbatim app landing page (styled like the app's UI)
- `eudaimonialabs.org/verbatim/privacy` — Verbatim privacy policy
- `eudaimonialabs.org/about` — About Eudaimonia Labs (future)

## User Experience Goals

- Each app's landing page should **feel native to the app** — using the app's own color palette and mocking the app's actual UI elements as design elements on the page.
- The company home page should be **beautiful, simple, and pragmatic** — describing the design philosophy and linking to apps.
- Privacy policies should be **complete, legally sufficient, and readable**.
- The whole site is a **static site** (Jekyll on GitHub Pages) — no server-side logic.
