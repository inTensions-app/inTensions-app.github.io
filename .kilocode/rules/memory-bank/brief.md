This is the centralized website for Eudaimonia Labs LLC. Its apex domain is `eudaimonialabs.org` online and it needs to host privacy policies for our current apps at their respective urls (e.g., `eudaimonialabs.org/<app_name>/privacy`), but also have an `about` page and other useful pages for getting to know our company. The idea is to have a centralized yet simple static site to provide a central directory to all our links and reference material.
